COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about NGO services.

NGO registration mein kitna time lagta hai?

Time organization type, document readiness, and government processing par depend karta hai. Consultation ke baad hum estimated timeline share karte hain.

Trust, Society aur Section 8 Company mein kya fark hai?

Inke governing laws, structure, and compliance alag hote hain. Aapke goals aur state requirements dekh kar suitable option choose kiya jata hai.

12A aur 80G kya hote hain?

Ye Income Tax Act ke tahat approvals hain; eligibility aur benefits aapki organization aur current rules par depend karte hain. Final advice qualified tax professional se verify karein.

FCRA registration kab zaroori hota hai?

Foreign contribution receive karne se pehle applicable FCRA approval aur rules check karna zaroori hai. Apni situation legal advisor se confirm karein.

NGO registration ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?

Requirements entity type aur jurisdiction ke hisaab se badalti hain. Aam taur par identity, address, governing document, and office proofs maange ja sakte hain.

Kya aap registration ke baad compliance mein bhi help karte hain?

Haan, annual reports, audit, tax filing aur related compliance services ke liye enquiry kar sakte hain. Scope engagement se pehle confirm hota hai.

Consultation kaise book karun?

Form submit karein ya phone/WhatsApp par contact karein. Team aapki requirement samajh kar next steps batayegi.
