Privacy Policy

Global NGO Services respects your privacy. This page explains the information that may be shared when you use this website or contact our team.

Information you provide

If you submit the consultation form, the website sends your name, 10-digit mobile number, and selected service to Web3Forms over HTTPS so our team can receive your enquiry. The site also provides direct WhatsApp, phone, email, and Instagram links; information you share through those services is handled under their own privacy policies.

If you submit a review, the website sends your name, rating, review text, and optional request to be contacted about publication to Web3Forms over HTTPS. If the submission fails, the page offers a WhatsApp fallback. A review is not published automatically.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries and provide requested consultancy information. Do not send sensitive identity, financial, or legal documents through this public form unless our team has given you a suitable secure method.

Storage and third-party services

The website stores your language preference in your browser's local storage when available. It loads Google Fonts and an illustrative image from third-party services. Web3Forms processes consultation and review submissions, and WhatsApp may be used if a form submission fails. These providers have their own terms and privacy practices.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-9FTRDCXHPM) loaded through Google Tag Manager (container GTM-MQ6JLN38) together with a direct Google tag. Analytics measures general website usage — such as pages visited and approximate session counts — to help us understand how visitors use the site and improve it over time.

The following privacy protections are in place for this website's analytics configuration:

  • IP anonymisation is enabled.
  • Google signals (cross-device reporting, advertising personalisation) are disabled.
  • Ad personalisation signals are disabled.
  • Form contents, contact details, link URLs, link text, and personally identifiable information are not sent to Analytics.
  • User-provided data collection and enhanced conversions are not enabled.
  • Outbound-click text and form-interaction content detection are not configured.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies — such as _ga and _ga_* — or similar browser storage to distinguish visits and sessions. Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. You can block or delete these cookies through your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

This website also fires lightweight analytics events — such as detecting a click on a phone, WhatsApp, or email link, or recording which service card a visitor viewed — to understand which services and contact methods are most used. No form text, personal information, or sensitive data is included in these events.

This version does not load Vercel Web Analytics or Speed Insights scripts.

Retention and your choices

Contact our team if you have a question about an enquiry you submitted or want to request its deletion. You can also clear the website's saved language preference through your browser settings.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features or applicable requirements change. Please review this page for the latest version.
