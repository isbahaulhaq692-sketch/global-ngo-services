Privacy Policy

Global NGO Services respects your privacy. This page explains the information that may be shared when you use this website or contact our team.

Information you provide

If you submit the consultation form, the website may process your name, 10-digit mobile number, and selected service. When configured, the form sends this information to Web3Forms to deliver your enquiry. The site also provides direct WhatsApp, phone, email, and Instagram links; information you share through those services is handled under their own privacy policies.

If you submit a review, the website may share your name, rating, review text, and your optional request to be contacted about publication through Web3Forms when configured, or WhatsApp otherwise. A review is not published automatically.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries and provide requested consultancy information. Do not send sensitive identity, financial, or legal documents through this public form unless our team has given you a suitable secure method.

Storage and third-party services

The website stores your language preference in your browser's local storage when available. It loads Google Fonts and an illustrative image from third-party services. If Web3Forms is enabled, its service processes consultation and review submissions; otherwise, review submissions can open in WhatsApp. These providers have their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

Contact our team if you have a question about an enquiry you submitted or want to request its deletion. You can also clear the website's saved language preference through your browser settings.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features or applicable requirements change. Please review this page for the latest version.
